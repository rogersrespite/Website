Meet the person behind the service

Hello, I'm Evan.

I'm Evan Rogers, a Registered Social Worker with a Master of Social Work. Through Rogers Respite and Community Services, I aim to provide welcoming, respectful support built around each person's interests, routines and individuality.

I believe respite can be more than a break in the day. It can create opportunities for connection, conversation and enjoyable activities while giving family caregivers time for their own needs.

How I can help

Support centred on people.

Flexible respite, community engagement and professional social work support for youth, adults, seniors and families, including people living with disabilities.

Companionship

One-on-one conversation, shared interests and engaging time together in a comfortable, respectful environment.

Community engagement

Meaningful activities and community outings based on personal interests, comfort and agreed-upon plans.

Caregiver respite

Dedicated time for caregivers to attend to appointments, errands, rest or other priorities.

Social work services

Supportive counselling, psychosocial needs assessment, care planning, advocacy, family support, referrals and help navigating community services and life transitions.

Scope: I do not provide psychotherapy.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Find answers to common questions about respite services, activities, packages and getting started.

Who can benefit from respite services?

Youth, adults and seniors who benefit from companionship or support, including people living with disabilities or dementia, and caregivers who need a break.

What activities can be included in a visit?

Activities are tailored to individual interests and needs and may include conversation, walks, recreational activities, scenic drives, and community outings.

How do the 6- and 9-hour packages work?

Respite and community support is $30/hour. Save $5 per hour with a 6-hour package ($150) or 9-hour package ($225). These packages do not apply to social work services ($60/hour).

How do I get started?

Start with a free 30-minute consultation to discuss your needs and whether the services are a good fit. Call 548-396-1616 or email evan@rogersrespite.ca.

Do you provide psychotherapy?

No. I do not provide psychotherapy. My social work services include supportive counselling, psychosocial support, care planning, advocacy and community resource navigation.
